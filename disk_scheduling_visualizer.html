How the algorithms work

FCFSServes requests in the order they arrive. Simple and fair, but may cause large movements.
SSTFChooses the request closest to the current head position. Can cause starvation.
SCANMoves in one direction to the disk end, then reverses direction. Also called the elevator algorithm.
C-SCANMoves in one direction to the end, jumps to the beginning, and continues in the same direction.
LOOKLike SCAN, but reverses at the last request instead of travelling to the physical disk end.
C-LOOKLike C-SCAN, but jumps between the last and first pending requests.

Note: For C-SCAN and C-LOOK, the wrap-around movement is included in total head movement. For SCAN and C-SCAN, the head travels to the physical disk boundary before reversing or wrapping.
